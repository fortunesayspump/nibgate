# Onchain escrow

For runs where the budget must be enforceable by contract rather than by promise, Dr. Nib escrows the cap on Arc using stock [ERC-8183 (Agentic Commerce)](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8183) plus a splitter contract. Testnet first; mainnet follows audit and soak.

## Roles

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| Client | your wallet — funds the job, receives refunds |
| Provider | the run's splitter contract — receives the full cap on completion, then divides it |
| Evaluator | the Nibgate keeper — attests completion against the run ledger |

## Lifecycle

1. **Open** — the backend opens the job (provider = splitter) after configure.
2. **Fund** — at review you sign `setBudget` + `approve` + `fund`. The full cap locks in escrow.
3. **Run** — stages meter spend in the offchain ledger, exactly as without escrow.
4. **Submit** — the worker submits `(reportHash, spentUsdc)` when the report lands.
5. **Complete** — the keeper completes with the ledger-attested spend.
6. **Split** — anyone executes the split: earned share minus 1% to the operator, 1% to treasury, remainder to your wallet — atomically, in one transaction.

Rejected or expired jobs refund in full with no fee. After expiry, `claimRefund` is permissionless: anyone can trigger it, so funds are never hostage to a backend being alive.
