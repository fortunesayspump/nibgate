# Dr. Nib research agent

Dr. Nib is a budgeted research agent that turns a question into a cited report. It visibly decides what evidence to pursue, whether another search round is worth its cost, and when to stop and write. You set the scope and a USDC cap before funding; the run page records its queries, source judgments, decisions, spending, and final report. Unspent escrow is returned at settlement.

When the evidence is thin, Dr. Nib can consider primary APIs or a priced source within the approved budget. A proposed paid call passes a separate judgment and spending checks before execution. A paid source is optional, not a requirement or a sign of better research. The activity feed shows these decisions and available payment receipts; the report discussion is a separate chat about the finished work.

Try it at **https://testnet.nibgate.xyz/dr-nib/research** with test USDC (wallet or Google sign-in required). The mainnet entry page directs new users to this preview until its funded research service is configured.

## The flow, end to end

```txt
composer → intake (batches ≤5, 8 questions max) → configure → review → approve → run → report
  topic      scoping Q&A + cap-hit label   depth/cap    plan +     escrow?    stages     cited + badge,
  sent       JEV stop/reframe              formats      estimate   then run   SSE live   matrix + receipt
```

 1. **Ask** — type the research question and hit Send. The thinking screen opens instantly with your message shown as sent; questions stream in when generated. A project is created straight away, so you can leave and resume later (`?project=`, or automatic for the last touched run). Nothing spends yet.
 2. **Intake, in batches** — up to 5 questions per screen (easy choices first), progress shown ("3 of 5 done"), prior batches collapsed with your answers labeled. Answer everything, submit once: one thinking per answer streams back, then you press Next when you've read. Intake caps at 8 questions total — if the judge still wants more, the cap wins and the transcript says `cap hit`. **Just plan it** skips the rest any time — unanswered questions stay unanswered, nothing is fabricated.
 3. **JEV runs the stop rule** — after each batch a calibrated decision picks `proceed` (brief is whole), `ask_more` (another batch), or `reframe` (the angle is dead — the next batch opens a different one, and the rejected direction stays on the record). The model proposes, JEV disposes; fallbacks are labeled, never silent.
 4. **Configure** — depth (quick/standard/deep), budget cap, live-web toggle, report length, optional creator tips, and [your own source files](./retrieval). Planning is free. Source-only mode requires a readable supplied file or link and disables external tools.
 5. **Review** — sub-questions (editable), uncertainties, cost estimate. Fund the [onchain escrow](./escrow) first — any amount from the estimate up to your cap; approve is then gated on it being Funded for at least the estimate.
 6. **Run** — the command-center page: report and sources on the left, the live activity feed on the right (tool calls with their results, queries, verdicts, your Q&A transcript, timestamps, jump-to-latest), guidance box pinned at the bottom. This is an audit of actions and stated reasons, not the model's private chain of thought. Research follows the approved scope; search results alone never trigger a claim that evidence disagrees. Pause/resume any time. Blips never wipe the page (stale banner + retry).
 7. **Report** — versioned, cited, with source mapping saved per report, sampled claim verification, report Q&A, Markdown-formatted free discussion, owner-only report links, seven export formats and a settlement receipt. Once the budget settles, use **Start fresh run** for further research with a new deposit; refunded money cannot fund a reprompt. Starting fresh copies the topic, so add your source files again.

## How a run spends

Research stages draw metered usage from the run's [budget](./budgets). Planning is free; the 1% platform fee comes from the operator's earned share and does not reduce the payer's refund. Web-enabled runs search configured indexes and providers, read pages and documents, score sources, and adapt the next round from evidence gaps. When retrieval is thin, a tool loop can try primary APIs, configured sandbox compute or paid sources. JEV owns trust and continue/write/stop decisions. Verification checks a bounded sample of claims; the badge is not a guarantee that every sentence was verified. Optional creator tips happen after writing and require eligible sources cited in the report.

## How the agent decides

The data stage is a propose → judge → execute loop with termination enforced in code, not in prompt prose: step/deadline/token/spend budgets, duplicate and stagnation detectors, an evidence checklist the run must cover, and a validated finish tool. Free reads run on deterministic policy; anything moving money always passes JEV, and a silent judge stops the run instead of running judgeless. Full ruleset: [Agent spending](./agent-spending). Enforcement map: [Spending gates](./gates).

## Machine access

The same runs are drivable by agents over MCP (`answer_question`, `answer_questions`, `approve_plan`, `reprompt_run`, `reconcile_run`, …): signed USDC payments for tips, paid unlocks, and x402 resources come from the run budget inside per-call ceilings, and every tool call is logged with what it cost and what came back. See the spending rules below and [Budgets & refunds](./budgets).

Source selection follows the approved brief, including restrictions such as official documentation only. A missing retrieved passage is reported as a gap in the collected evidence, not proof that the documentation or feature does not exist. Short custom reports use one concise writing pass; references keep stable citation numbers.

Reports show a claim-to-source matrix. When fewer than half of checked claims are confirmed, a prominent limited-evidence warning asks readers to review the sources before relying on the report. Unknown means the collected passage did not confirm the claim, not that the claim is false.
Verification covers a bounded sample of claims spread across the report's sections, not every sentence or an overall correctness score. Very long reports may exceed the verification pass's input limit and are labeled as partially covered in the activity record. Compare cost figures only when workload, units, environment, and assumptions match; a target transfer cost and a different workload's execution floor do not establish a cheaper network.
