# Private AI price verification addendum

Checked 16 October 2026 for the owner-only prototype budget gate. [Phala's model-specific page](https://phala.com/models/openai/gpt-oss-120b) identifies `openai/gpt-oss-120b` and lists $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.60 per million output tokens. These are 150,000 and 600,000 micro-USD per million tokens, respectively. [Phala's broader private-AI marketing page](https://phala.com/solutions/ai-agents) instead displays $0.10 per million input tokens for GPT OSS 120B. The model-specific page is the more precise reference, but these two Phala pages conflict. Neither page establishes a provider-side spending hard cap or resolves how hidden reasoning tokens, `max_tokens`, ACI routing, and possible additional fees are billed.

Do not treat the displayed prices as verified ceilings for a live job. Before enabling any paid call, check the actual account/route billing terms and any ACI surcharge in an official source or with Phala, set explicit price ceilings high enough to cover them, and confirm the worst-case reservation fits the AI subcap within the total $5/month infrastructure-plus-AI target. If these facts remain unclear, keep paid admission disabled. The current budget gate has no built-in default price, so it remains fail-closed until exact model-specific ceilings are configured.
