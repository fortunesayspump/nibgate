# Protected support judging: future patch only

Status on 2026-10-16: isolated, synthetic-tested code. It is **not** connected to guest HTTP, the current 300-word report release, MCP, or deployment. The current source-only report remains labelled **unverified**. The future-only files are `backend/src/privacy/private-verify-support-{core,store}.js`, `private-verify-support.js`, `private-support-flow-contract.js`, `private-verified-completion.js`, `private-support-session.js`, and their tests. They do not import ordinary JEV, Prisma singleton, eventlog, provider, memory, tools or the agent-lesson fallback.

## Narrow behavior and evidence

The existing measured extraction checkpoint identifies a bounded sample of factual claims and keeps them `unknown`. The new companion checks that completed sealed checkpoint, sealed run state revision 3, exactly one sealed source revision 1, sealed report version 1, original browser recipient, current run owner/status/version, and zero ordinary plaintext source or claim rows. For up to three sampled claims with a citation matching the sealed source URL, it finds the strongest 3,000-character source window with the same lexical-overlap approach as the existing `evidenceWindow`. An injected, branded `phala-aci-v1` AI port receives only that claim and passage inside the verified inference channel. It returns a strict finite probability in `[0,1]`; a local `0.6` threshold yields `supported` or `unknown`. Missing/uncited evidence remains `unknown` without a paid judge call. Deterministic word-shingle overlap is recorded with the sealed passage, but is not an originality verdict.

The stage has a separate `verify-support` step identity because extraction already owns `verify` attempt 1. A failed, active or expired support attempt cannot issue another paid call automatically. Once all model scores are in a sealed step checkpoint, a separate advisory transaction rechecks owner, run status/version, sealed state/source/report and extraction, and the exact checkpoint. It inserts every individual encrypted claim and a sealed commit manifest together, or none. An exact lost-response replay compares all sealed claims and returns fixed counts without another AI call. Nothing is written to ordinary `ResearchClaim.text/passages`; database writes, public step metadata and errors are fixed fields or ciphertext.

This is **model-scored, sampled support**, not JEV-equivalent verification. It checks at most three of the extracted claims; even a model score above threshold is not proof that a source establishes a fact. The output and claim-commit receipt therefore keep `verified:false` and are not offered as a user-visible verification badge. No support or originality claim should be made about uncited or unjudged statements.

This slice remains one uploaded source and the existing short (300-word) writer branch. The ordinary worker's web search/fetch, source scoring and cross-source synthesis, deeper research rounds, interactive guidance/resume, tool execution, JEV certification, long sectioned report, memory/agent lessons, exports and normal billing/settlement are **not** migrated by this patch. There is no automatic paid-call replay after a restart or lost response. A future guest journal must reconcile a durably completed ciphertext receipt before any new session is considered, using a rewrapped client-held state key when needed. Neither a fixed `complete` status nor a sampled support probability may be described as full Dr. Nib quality or whole-report verification.

## Required next-stage integration, in order

1. Keep today's opt-in `full-execute` source-only job unchanged and unverified. For a new, separately gated flow, reserve the *entire* worst-case model envelope before its first inference: one plan call, at most two short-writer calls, at most 24 extraction chunks from a 100,000-character report, and at most three support calls: **30 total**. Extraction's 10,000-character windows can split at paragraph boundaries just past the halfway point, so 100,000 characters can require more than ten calls; the code's hard maximum is 24. `private-support-flow-contract.js` enforces each stage's sublimit, hands the support runner a one-use child permit under the same reservation (never a second reservation), and requires the outer budget to settle before an audited completion callback runs. The existing run-unique `private-ai-budget.js` rejects 30 calls under its current maximum of 3; changing that policy, provider-side spending controls, verified pricing/usage and infrastructure metering requires separate review. Never pay for support after the existing completion.
2. Keep the run fixed `running` through internal report creation, claim extraction, support checkpoint and atomic claim commit. The separate, future-only `private-verified-completion.js` then rechecks the sealed plan/write/extraction/support lineage and claim set, report/source/state versions, original browser HPKE recipient and owner under lock, saves the bound HPKE ciphertext report and changes the run to fixed `complete` with a compare-and-swap. `private-support-session.js` composes the actual four stages and this completion with an injected attested transport and budget contract; it is callable in synthetic local tests, with no guest/HTTP import. The current `completeSourceOnlyRun` deliberately rejects extra verify steps and must not be weakened to accept them. A future full journal must reconcile a lost response to the same ciphertext receipt without re-inference.
3. Wire new stage calls only inside the measured guest, behind a separate flag and exact test-tenant allowlist. Use the client-held per-run key delivered to a fresh verified session and one budgeted, branded AI port for plan, write, extraction and support. Preserve fail-closed uncertain status on crash, provider failure, invalid score or key loss; there is no automatic restart of a paid call. Add a fixed private event kind only after its metadata and sealed replay contract are reviewed; never call ordinary eventlog.
4. On a disposable testnet environment, rehearse additive migrations `0007` and `0008`, rollback, browser rewrap, cold CVM restart, journal/status reconciliation, the 30-call admission at real price ceilings, and synthetic source/report canaries across database, R2, queue, HTTP, logs and traces. Keep the private report's user-facing status unverified until protected support scoring has a separately validated reliability standard and all wider Dr. Nib stages/tool egress are migrated.
